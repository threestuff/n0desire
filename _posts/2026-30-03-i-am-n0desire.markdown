Have you ever felt the need to become the very music you want to put out? This is what I will be talking about in this post as we go over embodying the spirit of music creation and become the I AM.

I have been making music for the last few years and it has been very on and off. I have enjoyed it truly as a hobby. The feeling of exporting a newly made song always had me going back, pending on the next song, or going over music video ideas in my head.

Thing is, i felt stumped by own thinking and what exactly i wanted from music. wohno (my artist name) did not necersiraly want to become famous and sign a record deal. Love for the art and passion for the craft was enough to keep me coming back for more.

skip to 2025 and playboi carti's I Am Music album is released. Hosted by Swamp Izzo. An atlantean DJ with history in the Hip Hop scene in the early 2010s.

This album sparked something within me. The SFX, the over-the-top vocal chants, the mixing on these songs, made me come to a realisation.

How much did i want to actually become the music i am creating. Other human beings are already doing the same thing. Why couldnt I take part in this opportunity.

i took the chance and will continue to do so.

speak soon.
